## Detailed Competitor Breakdown

### Nail Technician Exam Center (Tuli Education)
**The strongest direct competitor.**

- **Platform:** iOS + Android
- **Features:** Thousands of questions with detailed explanations; content inspired by Milady and Pivot Point methodologies; "Question I Got Wrong" area (mistake tracking); 5 State Board exams; offline support; no ads; FREE Question of the Day
- **Topics covered:** Infection Control, Anatomy & Physiology, Chemistry, Electricity, Manicuring, Nail Disorders & Diseases, Nail Product Chemistry, Nail Structure, Nail Tips and Wraps, Pedicuring, Salon Business, Skin Structure, UV Gels
- **Pricing:** $9.99/month subscription
- **Weaknesses:** No AI tutor, dated UI, no state-specific tailoring, no Vietnamese support
- **Notable:** Highly reviewed -- one user stated "This app is the reason I now have my license"

### Nail Technician Exam Prep 2026 (GetExam)

- **Platform:** iOS only
- **Features:** 1,500+ practice questions with detailed rationales; realistic exam simulation; updated for 2026 guidelines; works offline
- **Topics covered:** Nail anatomy, nail diseases and disorders, product chemistry, sanitation and safety practices, nail art and design, client consultation and customer service, salon business fundamentals
- **Pricing:** Subscription (exact price not confirmed)
- **Weaknesses:** No AI, no study guides, iOS only, no Android version, no Vietnamese support

### Nails Exam -- Luyen Thi Nails (LineCentury)

- **Platform:** iOS only
- **Features:** 900 exam-like questions; bilingual English + Vietnamese; advanced review technology; exam simulator (customizable time, questions, and topics)
- **Pricing:** Free/Paid model
- **Weaknesses:** Small question bank (only 900), no AI, iOS only, basic Vietnamese support
- **Notable:** Only competitor with Vietnamese support -- proves demand exists

### iPrep Nail Technician Exam Prep

- **Platform:** iOS + Android
- **Features:** Clean UI, structured learning, professional mobile app
- **Pricing:** Subscription
- **Weaknesses:** Smaller question bank; iPrep is a generic multi-category exam platform (not nail-specific); no AI

### PSI Nail Technician Theory Study-Pack

- **Platform:** Web only (browser-based, works on laptop/phone/tablet)
- **Features:** 350 sample questions following PSI's national content outlines; 90-day access
- **Pricing:** 90-day access subscription
- **Weaknesses:** Web only (no native app), limited features, small question bank (350), time-limited access

### Mometrix

- **Platform:** Web only
- **Features:** Free practice tests, study guides, flashcards; covers Scientific Concepts and Nail Technology Procedures sections
- **Pricing:** Free basic / paid premium study materials
- **Weaknesses:** Web only, not mobile-first, no native app experience

### Milady Exam Prep

- **Platform:** Mobile
- **Features:** Over 100,000 students have used Milady tools; major industry brand (textbook publisher)
- **Pricing:** $39.95 in-app purchase with 1 year of access
- **Weaknesses:** Broader cosmetology focus, not nail-specific

## Key Market Gaps

1. **No competitor has AI tutoring** -- Every single competitor is static question/answer. Not one offers conversational AI help.

2. **Poor state-specific experience** -- Most apps are generic "national exam" prep. None tailor the experience based on the user's specific state requirements.

3. **No smart study features** -- No spaced repetition, adaptive learning, or weak-area detection among any competitor. All are traditional flashcard/quiz formats.

4. **Bilingual is underserved** -- Only one app (Nails Exam) supports Vietnamese, and it is basic. 51.7% of nail techs are Asian (predominantly Vietnamese). This demographic is poorly served.

5. **No cross-platform dominance** -- Most competitors are iOS-only. Only Nail Technician Exam Center and iPrep have both iOS and Android. PSI and Mometrix are web-only.

6. **Low NIC exam pass rate (~55-60%)** -- Signals strong demand for better prep tools and creates a retaker market.
