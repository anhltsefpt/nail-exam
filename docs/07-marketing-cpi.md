## Keyword Competition Analysis

Nail exam keywords are on the cheaper side because:

- **Very low competition** -- only 5-6 apps competing for these keywords
- **Niche/long-tail keywords** -- terms like "nail technician exam prep" are specific, not broad
- **Small advertiser pool** -- unlike finance or gaming, few companies are bidding
- Education category average CPT is ~$1.24 (vs. Finance at $6.40 or Sports at $10.20)

### Campaign Structure Recommendation

Organize keywords into three buckets:
1. **Brand keywords** -- your app name (cheapest, highest conversion)
2. **Category keywords** -- "nail exam," "nail tech practice test" (moderate cost)
3. **Competitor keywords** -- competitor app names (can be effective; SoundCloud saw 58% conversion rate increase using custom product pages with competitor campaigns)

### Bidding Strategy

Once you have historical data, use:
> **Bid = Target CPA x Conversion Rate (CVR)**

## Vietnamese Keyword Opportunity

This is the single biggest marketing advantage:

- Vietnamese keywords like **"thi nail"** and **"luyen thi nail"** are estimated at **$0.50-$1.00 CPI** -- the cheapest of all target keywords
- **51.7% of all nail technicians** are Asian, with Vietnamese being the largest subgroup
- Vietnamese language support could capture a large share of this underserved demographic
- Only one competitor (Nails Exam) serves this market, and it's basic
- "This is where your multilingual advantage directly translates to lower acquisition costs"

## Channel Recommendations

### 1. Apple Search Ads (Recommended Starting Point)

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Estimated CPI | $1.50-$3.50 |
| Pricing model | Cost-per-tap (Advanced) or Cost-per-install (Basic) |
| Best for | High-intent users searching for exam prep |
| Education category CPT | ~$1.24 |

**Apple Ads Basic** lets you define a max CPI you're willing to pay. Apple provides suggested max CPI based on your app and competitive benchmarks. Good for starting out.

**Apple Ads Advanced** gives full control over keyword bidding, ad placements, and audience targeting. Move here once you have baseline data.

### Timing Consideration

Education app CPTs may spike after summer, tied to back-to-school and exam season timing. Nail tech exams don't follow a strict academic calendar, but awareness of seasonal CPI fluctuations helps with budget planning.
