# Report: Types of Nail Technician Exams & Estimated Candidate Distribution

## Executive Summary

There are **5 distinct exam pathway types** for nail technician licensing across the 50 US states + DC. The majority of candidates (~60-65%) take a **Written + Practical** exam combination. Here is the full breakdown:

| Exam Pathway | # of States | Est. % of Annual Exam Candidates | Est. Annual Attempts |
|-------------|-------------|----------------------------------|---------------------|
| Type 1: NIC Written + NIC Practical | ~25 states | ~40-45% | ~14,000-18,000 |
| Type 2: Written Only (no practical) | ~5-7 states | ~25-30% | ~9,000-12,000 |
| Type 3: State-Specific Written + Practical | ~15 states | ~20-25% | ~7,000-10,000 |
| Type 4: No State Exam (training only) | 2 states (FL + possibly others) | ~8-10% | 0 (no exam) |
| Type 5: No License Required | 1 state (CT) | ~1% | 0 (no exam) |

**Total estimated exam attempts per year: ~35,000-45,000** (includes retakes; ~25,000-30,000 unique candidates)

---

## Type 1: NIC Written + NIC Practical (Both Required)

**The most common pathway.** ~25 states use the NIC exam system and require candidates to pass BOTH the written theory exam and a hands-on practical exam.

### Exam Format

**Theory (Written) Exam:**
- 110 multiple-choice items (100 scored + 10 unscored pretest)
- 90 minutes
- 75% passing score
- Computer-based at testing centers (PSI, Prov, or Prometric)

**Practical Exam:**
- Live demonstration on a model or mannequin
- 6 core domain sections based on national job analysis
- Evaluated by trained NIC raters
- Must use disinfecting WIPES only (spray banned since Jan 1, 2020)

### States Using This Pathway

AL, AZ, AR, DE, GA, HI, ID, IA, KS, LA, ME, MN, MO, MS, MT, NH, NJ, NM, NC, ND, NV, OH, OK, PA, RI, SC, SD, UT, VT, VA, WA, WI, WV, WY

### Key States by Size (Employment in Nail Industry)

| State | Nail Industry Workers | Training Hours | Notes |
|-------|----------------------|----------------|-------|
| Georgia | Large | 525 hrs | PSI-administered, 70% passing score |
| Virginia | Medium-Large | 150 hrs | EN/VI/KO/ES/RU languages, PCS-administered |
| Ohio | Medium | 200 hrs | 110 MCQ, 90 min |
| North Carolina | Medium | 300 hrs | Prov-administered |
| Pennsylvania | Medium | 200 hrs | PSI-administered |

### Estimated Candidate Volume

- These ~25 states represent roughly 40-45% of all nail exam candidates
- Annual estimate: ~14,000-18,000 exam attempts (theory + practical combined)
- These are mostly smaller/mid-sized states by nail industry employment
- Higher total cost to candidates (two separate exams, ~$100-150 combined fees)

## Type 2: Written Exam Only (No Practical)

A growing category. Several states have dropped the practical exam requirement, keeping only the written theory portion.

### States

| State | Exam System | Training Hours | Details |
|-------|-------------|----------------|---------|
| **California** | NIC Written | 400 hrs | Practical eliminated Jan 1, 2022. 60 scored + 10 pretest, 90 min, 75% pass. PSI-administered. EN/KO/ES/VI/ZH. |
| **Illinois** | PSI Written | 350 hrs | Computer-based MCQ only, no practical. Covers theory AND practical topics in written form. |
| **Nebraska** | NIC Written | 300 hrs | NIC written satisfies both written and practical requirements. |
| **Alaska** | Written | 12 hrs (course) | Written exam for Manicurist License. |

### Why This Category Matters Most for the App

**California alone may represent 20-25% of all US nail exam candidates.** California has:
- The largest nail industry workforce in the US (~56,200 workers in nail salons/personal care, 27% of national total)
- 5 exam languages: English, Korean, Spanish, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese
- High Vietnamese population (huge target market)
- High turnover and continuous inflow of new technicians

### Estimated Candidate Volume

- ~5-7 states, but California alone is massive
- Annual estimate: ~9,000-12,000 exam attempts
- California likely accounts for 7,000-9,000 of those attempts
- Lower barrier to entry (one exam, not two) → higher first-attempt numbers but also attracts more candidates

## Type 3: State-Specific Exams (Not NIC)

Some states develop and administer their own nail technician exams rather than using NIC. These may still be administered by PSI, Prov, or other vendors, but the content is state-developed.

### States

| State | Exam Format | Training Hours | Details |
|-------|-------------|----------------|---------|
| **Texas** | State Written + State Practical | 600 hrs | PSI-administered. Written includes: Nail Tips & Wraps (17%), Acrylic & Gel (15%), Licensing & Regulation (9%). EN/ES/VI (call PSI for ES/VI). |
| **New York** | State Written + State Practical | 250 hrs | DOS-administered. $40 application + $15/exam. 4-year license. Trainee path available. |
| **Colorado** | State Written + State Practical | 600 hrs | PSI-administered state exams. |
| **Oregon** | State exam | 350 hrs | Oregon-specific practical and written. |
| **Maryland** | State exam | 250 hrs | Theory + practical. |
| Others | Varies | Varies | Several additional states write their own exams |

### Key Insight: Texas

Texas is the **2nd largest state by nail industry employment** (~39,700 workers) and uses its OWN exam, not NIC. This makes Texas a significant standalone market segment. The Texas exam has a unique content breakdown that differs from NIC domains.

### Estimated Candidate Volume

- ~15 states using non-NIC exams
- Annual estimate: ~7,000-10,000 exam attempts
- Texas likely accounts for 4,000-6,000 of those
- New York likely accounts for 1,500-2,500

## Type 4: No State Exam Required (Training Completion Only)

### States

| State | Training Hours | Details |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| **Florida** | 180 hrs | No state exam. Complete school program + 4-hr HIV/AIDS course → apply for COSMO 1 license. $70 total fees. |

Florida is the **3rd largest state by nail industry employment** (~33,900 workers). Despite having no state exam, these candidates still need to complete training and may still use exam prep apps for school exit exams and knowledge verification.

### Estimated Candidate Volume

- 1-2 states (Florida is the main one; Illinois is sometimes categorized here but does have a written exam)
- Annual estimate: ~3,000-4,000 new licenses issued (no exam attempts)
- These candidates are still potential app users for study/practice, even though there's no state exam

## Candidate Distribution Analysis

### By State Employment (Proxy for Exam Volume)

The nail salon & personal care industry workforce by state provides the best proxy for where exam candidates are concentrated:

| State | Nail Industry Workers (2023) | % of National | Exam Type | Est. Annual Exam Attempts |
|-------|------------------------------|---------------|-----------|--------------------------|
| **California** | 56,200 | ~27% | Written Only (NIC) | 7,000-9,000 |
| **Texas** | 39,700 | ~19% | State Written + Practical | 4,000-6,000 |
| **Florida** | 33,900 | ~16% | **No Exam** | 0 (3,000-4,000 new licenses) |
| **New York** | ~17,000 | ~8% | State Written + Practical | 1,500-2,500 |
| **Georgia** | Large | ~4% | NIC Written + Practical | 800-1,200 |
| **Virginia** | Medium-Large | ~3% | NIC Written + Practical | 600-1,000 |
| **Illinois** | Medium | ~3% | Written Only (PSI) | 600-900 |
| **Ohio** | Medium | ~3% | NIC Written + Practical | 500-800 |
| All Others | ~17% | ~17% | Various | 4,000-6,000 |

### Key Takeaway: The "Big 4" States

**California, Texas, Florida, and New York together represent ~70% of the nail industry workforce** and therefore ~70% of potential app users. Exam prep strategy should prioritize:

1. **California** (Written Only, NIC, 5 languages) -- largest single market
2. **Texas** (State-specific Written + Practical, EN/ES/VI) -- 2nd largest
3. **Florida** (No Exam, but training content needed) -- 3rd largest
4. **New York** (State-specific Written + Practical, EN only) -- 4th largest

## Data Sources & Limitations

**What we know with confidence:**
- NIC exam structure, domains, and weights (from official CIBs)
- Which states use NIC vs. state-specific exams (from NIC, state boards)
- BLS employment data: 210,100 jobs in 2024, 24,800 annual openings
- State-level industry workforce (from Data USA/Census: CA 56.2k, TX 39.7k, FL 33.9k)
- NIC exam pass rate estimate: ~55-60%
- Total estimated exam attempts: 25,000-40,000/year (from doc 01-app-overview.md)

**What is estimated / needs verification:**
- Per-state exam attempt volumes (NIC and state boards do not publicly publish this data)
- The exact number of states in each exam type category (some states are ambiguous or have changed recently)
- Precise candidate distribution percentages across exam types
- Florida and Illinois classification (FL clearly has no exam; IL has written-only)

**Recommended next steps to get precise data:**
- Contact NIC directly for annual exam volume statistics
- Request California BBC quarterly statistical reports from https://www.barbercosmo.ca.gov/about_us/reports.shtml
- Review CA BBC 2026 Sunset Review Report (published at https://www.barbercosmo.ca.gov/forms_pubs/sunset_2026.pdf) for multi-year exam statistics
- Contact PSI Services for aggregate exam administration data
