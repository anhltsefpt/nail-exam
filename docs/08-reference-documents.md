## 1. NIC Official Exam Resources

The National-Interstate Council of State Boards of Cosmetology (NIC) develops and administers the standardized nail technician exam used by 33+ states (38 states contracted total).

- **NIC Website:** https://nictesting.org/
- **Examination Information:** https://nictesting.org/examination-information/

### Theory Examination CIB (Candidate Information Bulletin)

The Theory CIB defines the exam structure and content outline. This is the single most important document for building the question bank.

**Exam Structure:**

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total items | 110 (100 scored + 10 unscored pretest) |
| Time allowed | 90 minutes |
| Passing score | 75% |
| Format | Multiple choice |
| Languages | English, Korean, Russian, Spanish, Vietnamese |
| Sections | Scientific Concepts (40%) + Nail Technology Procedures (60%) |

**8-Domain Content Outline with Weights:**

| Domain | Topic | Weight |
|--------|-------|--------|
| I | Infection Control & Safety Practices | 15% |
| II | Anatomy & Physiology | 15% |
| III | Chemistry of Nail Products | 10% |
| IV | Client Consultation & Documentation | 5% |
| V | Nail Service Tools | 5% |
| VI | Nail Service Preparation | 5% |
| VII | Manicure & Pedicure Services | 25% |
| VIII | Nail Enhancement Services (Application/Maintenance/Removal) | 20% |
| | **Total** | **100%** |

**Key Practical Exam Rules (effective Jan 1, 2020):**
- Only viricidal, bactericidal, fungicidal disinfecting **wipes** allowed (spray disinfectant banned)
- Monomer must be low-odor, in original container with manufacturer's label
- Mannequin hands (if required) must be entire hand with non-removable digits

### Notable State Details

- **California:** 60 scored + 10 pretest questions, 90 min, 75% to pass. Administered by PSI. Apprenticeship option: 3,200 hrs full-time + 220 classroom hrs over 2 years.
- **Texas:** Written exam includes Nail Tips & Wraps (17%, 15 questions), Acrylic & Gel Nails (15%, 14 questions), Licensing & Regulation (9%, 8 questions). Must call PSI for Spanish/Vietnamese exams.
- **New York:** Application fee $40, exam fee $15 each. Trainee path available: 1 year as Licensed Nail Trainee + 27-hour course + exams. 1-hour Domestic Violence & Sexual Assault Awareness course required (since June 2020).
- **Florida:** No state exam -- school exit exam only. 4-hour HIV/AIDS course required. Application fee ~$75. Renewal every 2 yrs (Oct 31 odd years), $45 fee.
- **Georgia:** Min score 70%. Testing fee $109 combined ($64 practical + $45 theory). Apprenticeship: 1,050 hours. Minimum age 17.
- **Ohio:** 110 multiple-choice questions, 90 min. Minimum age 16, 10th grade education. Application fee $40.
- **Virginia:** Apprenticeship option: 2,000 hours. Minimum age 16, no HS diploma required. Administered by Professional Credential Services (PCS).

### Cosmetology Licensure Compact

Interstate compact being adopted by states. Virginia among first 10 states to enact legislation. Expected to activate early 2026.

## 7. Recommended Content Architecture

### Phase 1 -- MVP

- **Question bank** covering all 8 NIC domains with correct weight distribution
- **Bilingual support:** English + Vietnamese
- **Timed mock exams** matching real exam format (110 questions, 90 minutes)
- **State selector** to tailor content and requirements

### Phase 2

- **Practical exam prep** (step-by-step procedures, checklists, video guidance)
- **Spanish language** support
- **State-specific law/regulation questions** for top states

### Phase 3

- **Continuing education courses** for license renewal
- **Additional languages** (Korean, Russian, Simplified Chinese)
- **Video content** (procedural demonstrations)
- **Adaptive learning** (spaced repetition, weak-area detection)
